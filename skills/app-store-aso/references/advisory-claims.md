# Advisory Claims

This file preserves useful upstream ASO ideas without making them local rules.
Use these claims only as hypotheses unless the user supplies current reliable
evidence.

## Keep Advisory

- "June 2025 screenshot caption OCR indexing" and any claim that screenshot
  captions are a first-tier App Store ranking factor.
- Specific ranking-weight percentages, such as title keywords ranking a fixed
  percentage higher than other metadata.
- Conversion multipliers, including app preview "2x" or icon/screenshot
  percentage uplifts, unless tied to a cited Apple developer story or the
  user's own analytics.
- Claims that the new app boost was eliminated.
- Fixed update cadences such as "update every 2-4 weeks" as an algorithm rule.
- Claims that descriptions, What's New text, IAP names, or in-app events have
  a particular ranking weight beyond Apple-published behavior.
- Rating thresholds, review response uplift numbers, and retention/download
  velocity ranking formulas.
- Custom Product Page organic keyword performance claims beyond Apple's
  documented keyword assignment and visibility behavior.

## How To Use Advisory Claims Safely

- Phrase them as "test this" or "monitor this", not "Apple ranks this way".
- Put them under "Advisory hypotheses to test" in reports.
- Ask for source data when the recommendation depends on rank, popularity,
  conversion, or competitor behavior.
- Prefer Apple-documented behavior and user-supplied analytics over industry
  claims.

## Preserved Upstream Ideas

- Metadata hierarchy thinking: app name, subtitle, and keywords are important
  discovery surfaces.
- Keyword diversity and cross-field combination thinking.
- Screenshot messaging should reinforce the metadata promise.
- App previews, icons, CPPs, PPO tests, and in-app events can improve
  conversion or audience fit when measured.
- Ratings/reviews, localization, and update cadence can be operational levers,
  but their ranking effects need evidence.
