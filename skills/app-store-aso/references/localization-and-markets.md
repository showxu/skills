# Localization And Markets

Use this reference when ASO involves multiple locales, storefronts, or market
coverage.

## Principles

- Localize app description, keywords, app previews, and screenshots for each
  market where the app is offered.
- Do not just translate English keywords. Research local search terms,
  competitor language, category vocabulary, and cultural expectations.
- Keep metadata truthful and culturally appropriate. Avoid local claims,
  certifications, awards, regulated statements, or availability promises
  without evidence.
- Validate byte limits per locale. Non-ASCII keywords can hit the 100-byte
  limit sooner than 100 visible characters.

## Audit Signals

- Non-primary locales with identical keyword fields usually indicate a missed
  localization opportunity.
- Latin-script keywords in non-Latin locales can be valid when users search
  that way; do not remove them automatically.
- Right-to-left languages need screenshot and copy layout review.
- Text expansion can break screenshot captions and promotional text.
- Locale-specific screenshots should match the localized metadata promise.

## Market Prioritization

Use user-supplied evidence when choosing markets:

- current impressions, downloads, conversion, revenue, or retention by country
- competitor strength by storefront
- language fit for the app category
- support readiness and regulatory constraints
- localization cost and maintenance burden

Without evidence, recommend a research plan rather than claiming priority
markets as fact.
