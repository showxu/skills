# Campaign Preflight

Use this reference before launching or changing paid acquisition campaigns.

## Scope

Record:

- platform and account
- campaign objective
- conversion event
- campaign, ad group, ad set, or keyword structure
- geography and language
- device or placement scope
- schedule
- budget and bid strategy
- landing page or store listing
- creative assets and copy
- owner and approval status

## Budget Safety

- Confirm whether the budget is daily, lifetime, campaign-level, ad-set-level,
  or account-level.
- Confirm currency, start date, end date, and billing account.
- Identify whether any change can increase spend immediately.
- For tests, define a stop condition and review date.
- Do not rely on rough "small budget" wording. Record the exact amount.

## Structure Checks

- Does the campaign structure support the intended measurement?
- Are brand, category, competitor, discovery, remarketing, or broad-audience
  ideas separated when useful?
- Are negative keywords or exclusions needed to control overlap?
- Are geographies and languages aligned with the landing page or store page?
- Does the campaign objective match the conversion event?

## Creative / Landing Page Consistency

- Ad promise matches landing page or store listing promise.
- CTA matches the conversion event.
- Pricing, offers, claims, and availability match the destination.
- Creative dimensions and text meet platform requirements.
- Restricted claims are flagged before submission.
