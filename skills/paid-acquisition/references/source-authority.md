# Source Authority

Last checked: 2026-04-30.

Use current official platform documentation and live account UI before
treating paid acquisition settings, policies, budget behavior, or tracking
flows as final.

## Official Sources

Apple Ads:

- Policies: https://ads.apple.com/policies
- Campaign structure: https://ads.apple.com/app-store/best-practices/campaign-structure
- Keywords: https://ads.apple.com/app-store/help/keywords/0014-add-and-manage-keywords

Google Ads:

- Policies: https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6008942?hl=en
- Website conversion setup:
  https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9119707

Meta ads:

- Use the current Meta Business Help Center or Meta policy pages directly in
  the browser/account when a task depends on Meta-specific settings, budget
  behavior, or policy enforcement. Do not rely on remembered Meta UI behavior.

## Authority Tiers

- Official policy docs and current live UI/API behavior are rule sources.
- Platform best-practice docs are strong operational guidance but still need
  account-context judgment.
- Agency playbooks, Reddit posts, blogs, and benchmark lists are advisory.
- User account exports are evidence for that account, not universal platform
  rules.

## Verification Rule

Before live changes, verify:

- account and campaign identifiers
- current settings in UI/API
- current budget and billing scope
- current tracking/conversion status
- policy or review warnings
- exact change requested by the user
