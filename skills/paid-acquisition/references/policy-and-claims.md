# Policy And Claims

Use this reference for advertising policy and claim-risk preflight.

## Always Check

- product category and restricted verticals
- geography-specific restrictions
- landing page disclosures and functionality
- app store listing consistency
- trademark or competitor terms
- pricing, discount, ranking, award, or scarcity claims
- privacy, health, finance, legal, safety, children, employment, housing,
  credit, gambling, alcohol, pharmaceutical, political, or sensitive content

## Claim Handling

- Factual product claims need visible destination proof.
- Comparative and superlative claims need strong evidence.
- Offers need current pricing, dates, and eligibility details.
- Regulated claims need qualified review.
- User attributes in ad copy can create policy risk; review platform rules
  before using sensitive personalization.

## Escalation

Escalate instead of approving when:

- the category is restricted or regulated
- the destination page lacks required disclosure
- the ad references sensitive personal attributes
- the claim depends on medical, financial, legal, safety, or compliance
  evidence
- the platform has a policy warning or account limitation
