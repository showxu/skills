# Localization

## Workflow

1. Draft the primary locale first.
2. Get approval on meaning and claims.
3. Adapt each target locale naturally.
4. Recount each locale against the 4000-character limit.
5. Flag any locale that needs product-owner review.

## Rules

- Translate meaning, not word order.
- Avoid literal idiom translation.
- Preserve feature names only when they are brand or product terms.
- Use a professional tone suitable for an App Store product page.
- Respect local formality where the language distinguishes formal and informal
  address.
- Shorten before translation when the source is already close to the limit.
- Do not keyword-stuff localized notes.

## Expansion Risk

German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, and Russian often expand compared with
English. Chinese and Japanese may be shorter but still need natural phrasing.
Do not assume a locale fits because the source locale fits.
