# Storyboard And Copy

## Core Principle

Google Play preview assets should make the app or game experience clear
quickly. Treat screenshots and feature graphics as listing assets, but keep
them grounded in the actual product.

## Screenshot Narrative

- Lead with the strongest core value proposition.
- Use one user-visible idea per screenshot.
- Prefer actual in-app or in-game screens over abstract marketing art.
- Vary layouts so the set does not look mechanically repeated.
- Use phone, tablet, and large-screen captures that match the supported device
  experience instead of stretching one screenshot across every device type.
- For landscape tablet assets, prefer caption-left plus product-right layouts
  when designing framed marketing screenshots.

## Feature Graphic

The feature graphic is a 1024x500 banner-like asset. It may appear as a
preview video cover and in larger Play surfaces.

Plan it as:

- app/game name or brand signal
- concise value proposition
- one strong visual or product moment
- center-safe focal point
- no fragile edge text or critical content in likely cutoff zones

Avoid:

- price or sale claims
- "best", "#1", "top", "new", "free", "million downloads"
- Google Play badges or other store badges
- third-party trademarks without permission
- device imagery that will age or be cropped awkwardly
- tiny UI details that disappear on small screens

## Copy Rules

- Keep text short enough to read at thumbnail size.
- Avoid keyword stuffing and call-to-action copy such as "download now".
- Do not use ranking, award, testimonial, price, or promotional claims unless
  the user provides current evidence and the claim is allowed.
- Keep screenshot and short-description messaging complementary. Google
  recommends avoiding redundant messaging across listing assets.
- Use alt text for each uploaded graphic asset; keep it concrete and under
  140 characters.

## Bad To Better

| Weak | Better | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Download now and save time | Plan your day faster | no CTA, clearer benefit |
| Best budget tracker | See where money goes | avoids unsupported ranking |
| AI tools and reminders | Turn notes into tasks | one idea, outcome-first |
| Free sale this week | Build habits that last | avoids price/time-sensitive copy |
