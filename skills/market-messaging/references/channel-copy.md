# Channel Copy

Use this reference when adapting a message to a concrete market surface.

## Store Listing

- Lead with the product category or core job before secondary features.
- Keep claims consistent with screenshots, metadata, reviews, and app
  behavior.
- Use store-specific metadata skills for field limits, keyword behavior, and
  live listing operations.
- Avoid stuffing every feature into the first paragraph. Use hierarchy:
  promise, proof, features, trust, CTA.

## Screenshot Copy

- One screenshot, one job.
- Use short headlines that can survive localization and small screens.
- Match the headline to visible UI in the image.
- Avoid claims the screenshot cannot demonstrate.
- Use the asset-specific skill for dimensions, device frames, export, and
  upload readiness.

## Landing Page

- Put audience, promise, and proof above detailed feature lists.
- Use sections to answer objections: what it does, who it is for, why trust
  it, how it works, what happens next.
- Keep CTA language tied to the next action, not a vague value claim.
- SEO work belongs in `organic-search`; this file only covers message clarity.

## Launch Copy

- Explain what changed, why it matters, and who benefits.
- Prefer specific shipped outcomes over "excited to announce" filler.
- Do not overpromise roadmap items unless the surface is explicitly about
  roadmap communication.
- Connect launch messaging to store, web, and screenshot copy so users see the
  same promise across surfaces.

## Ad Inputs

- Provide hooks, proof points, audience, objections, and safe claims.
- Campaign setup, targeting, budgets, policies, tracking, and live account
  operations belong in `paid-acquisition`.

## Review Checklist

- Does the copy identify the product category quickly?
- Is the primary benefit visible before feature detail?
- Is every claim supportable from supplied evidence?
- Is terminology consistent across channels?
- Does the CTA match the user's likely next step?
- Does the surface need a platform-specific field, policy, or asset check?
