# Claims And Proof

Use this reference before publishing or reviewing claims that could mislead,
require evidence, or create compliance risk.

## Claim Classes

- Factual claim: describes shipped behavior or available features.
- Outcome claim: says users will save time, earn money, reduce risk, learn
  faster, improve health, or get a measurable result.
- Comparative claim: says the product is better, faster, cheaper, more secure,
  simpler, or more accurate than an alternative.
- Superlative claim: says best, fastest, easiest, number one, leading, or
  most trusted.
- Regulated claim: touches health, finance, legal, safety, children, privacy,
  employment, housing, credit, gambling, alcohol, pharmaceuticals, or other
  restricted categories.
- Time-sensitive claim: mentions current pricing, limited offers, availability,
  ranking, awards, or version-specific behavior.

## Evidence Levels

- Direct product evidence: screenshot, demo, code behavior, release notes, or
  documentation supplied by the user.
- User evidence: reviews, support tickets, interviews, or customer quotes.
- Measurement evidence: analytics, benchmark, A/B test, conversion report, or
  ranking tracker output.
- Third-party evidence: award, certification, press quote, independent review,
  or platform badge.
- No evidence: claim must be softened, reframed, or marked as needing proof.

## Rewrite Rules

- Replace unsupported superlatives with specific behavior.
- Replace guaranteed outcomes with capability or intent.
- Replace vague "AI-powered" or "smart" claims with the actual user-visible
  job the system performs.
- For regulated or sensitive categories, identify risk and require qualified
  review instead of approving copy.
- When proof exists but is narrow, keep the claim narrow.

## Output Notes

Call out claims as:

```text
Supported:
- ...

Needs evidence:
- ...

Risky / requires qualified review:
- ...

Safer rewrite:
- ...
```
