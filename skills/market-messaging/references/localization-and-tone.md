# Localization And Tone

Use this reference when adapting market messaging across locales or checking
tone for public channels.

## Localization Workflow

1. Identify the source message, target locale, channel, and hard constraints.
2. Preserve the user outcome, not the exact sentence structure.
3. Check whether the category term, pain point, CTA, and proof make sense in
   the target market.
4. Re-check length, line breaks, screenshots, button-like copy, and claims.
5. Mark idioms, cultural references, comparative claims, and regulated claims
   for local review.

## Tone Rules

- Use direct, concrete language over hype.
- Match the product category: productivity copy can be restrained; games and
  entertainment can be more expressive.
- Keep confidence proportional to proof.
- Avoid humor when the product touches safety, finance, health, privacy,
  migration, account recovery, or high-stakes decisions.

## Length And Layout

- Expect translated copy to expand. Plan shorter source lines for screenshots,
  cards, app store fields, and ad inputs.
- Do not solve overflow by making text unreadable. Rewrite the message.
- For RTL locales, review layout, punctuation, numbers, product names, and
  mixed-language terms in context.
